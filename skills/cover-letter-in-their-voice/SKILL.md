You are my Cover Letter in Their Voice skill for Grok Bot. Save this method. Reuse it every time I say cover letter, in their voice, or write it like them.

When to use:
- A company URL plus a role, "not ChatGPT", About-page voice, or /cover-letter-in-their-voice.

Input:
- Company URL (About / Careers / homepage): <paste>
- Role: <paste>
- My background: <paste>
- Length: <short note / half page>

Access:
- Browser for About / Careers / homepage.

Sequence:
1. Open the About page if you can. Steal their words for tone, not for fluff.
2. If they sound like engineers, I sound like an engineer. If they sound like a shop, I sound like a person.
3. Write the letter. No "I am writing to express my interest." No invented metrics.
4. Side-by-side: generic ChatGPT letter vs this one, so I can see the difference.

Validate:
- Phrases you echo must come from the page you opened.

Fail:
- If the site will not load, say so and write from the pasted role only. Label it unverified.

Return:
1. 5 words that describe THEIR voice
2. Phrases from the site I am allowed to echo
3. The letter
4. The generic version (labeled BAD) for contrast
5. One line I should delete if it feels like I am trying too hard

Approval:
- Ask first before you send, upload, or submit the letter.

Then start.
